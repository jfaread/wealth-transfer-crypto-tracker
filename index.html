    Calculation and time ranges

    Return = (price ÷ initial price − 1) × 100. The first observation is the opening price of the first candle; subsequent observations are candle closes. The final candle can still be in progress, and its price can update while requests are being fetched. The latest ticks across coins are not precisely synchronised. No fees, staking income or inflation adjustments are included.

    One day and five days are rolling periods. Months and years are calendar offsets. YTD begins on 1 January (UTC). Start times are rounded down to the sampling interval, so displayed periods are approximate. The exact dates appear above the graph. Zoom does not reset the return baseline.

    Transparent data. Independent code.

    Periods up to one year use CoinGecko aggregate USD prices for every coin. The original five coins’ five-year view uses Binance USDT prices, with the source and currency labelled. Free aggregate history is limited to 365 days; unavailable or pre-launch periods are not invented. The clearly labelled saved one-day fallback is CoinGecko data in USD from 13 November 2026. Aggregate baselines use the first observation within one sampling interval of the period start; exact observation timestamps are available on inspection and in CSV exports.

    News, privacy and refresh behaviour

    Public market data is requested directly from Binance’s market-data API. No API key, wallet, account or sign-in is required. The data provider receives ordinary connection information such as your IP address. Availability may depend on location and provider service limits.

    The shared aggregate price cache refreshes hourly through this repository’s free public GitHub Actions workflow; its snapshot timestamp is displayed. Browser prices and publisher headlines are checked every five minutes while the page is visible and auto-refresh is on. Free feed providers may cache results longer, so a check is not a guarantee of a new headline. No ChatGPT task, server schedule or AI API is used. No check runs after this page is closed. RSS feeds are retrieved by this standalone server, or the keyless rss2json service on static hosting; provider caching and rate limits apply. This page stores only its own cached market data and settings under wtct:v3: keys. It adds no analytics, cookies or service workers. Cached data is visibly dated; failed requests never produce fabricated values.
